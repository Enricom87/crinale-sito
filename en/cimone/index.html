❓ Cimone: frequently asked questions

Cimone: how much snow is forecast?

Over the next 5 days our calculation expects about 1 cm at the top (Monte Cimone, 2165 m) and 1 cm at the base (Lago della Ninfa, 1500 m). The first snowfall is expected on Saturday 28 November.

Cimone: at what altitude does it snow?

Saturday 28 November the snow level is expected around 1000 m. We calculate it from the wet-bulb temperature, the effect of heavy precipitation and the cold air pooling in hollows.

How often are the forecasts updated?

Five times a day, with the latest model runs. The first 5 days come from a physical calculation built for the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, the following days from about 300 scenarios from the main weather centres.

Measured snow is the rise in snow depth: wind and settling can make it look lower than what fell. We count a forecast as right if it's within 3 cm or 40%.

🎯 Calibration in progress: automatic correction starts after at least 4 compared snowfalls (3 so far).

Our take

What's coming in the next 3 days: a dusting up high, 1 cm at 2165 m and 1 cm at 1500 m, with the snow level around 1000 m. For today and tomorrow we trust the most detailed models (ICON-2I, ICON-CH2, AROME, ICON-D2), the ones that see each valley individually.

And after that? From day 4 to day 10 the big models see no snowfall worth mentioning.

On provincial and local roads the owner's order applies: the requirement is always signposted.
