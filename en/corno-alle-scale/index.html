Corno alle Scale: how much snow is forecast?

Over the next 5 days our calculation expects about 1 cm at the top (Corno alle Scale, 1945 m) and 1 cm at the base (Cavone, 1422 m). The first snowfall is expected on Saturday 28 November.

Corno alle Scale: at what altitude does it snow?

Saturday 28 November the snow level is expected around 1000 m. We calculate it from the wet-bulb temperature, the effect of heavy precipitation and the cold air pooling in hollows.

How often are the forecasts updated?

Five times a day, with the latest model runs. The first 5 days come from a physical calculation built for the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, the following days from about 300 scenarios from the main weather centres.
