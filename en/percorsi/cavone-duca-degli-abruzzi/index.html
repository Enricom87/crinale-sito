What you'll find up there

  • Little fresh snow forecast up high (about 2 cm in 3 days).
  • This site doesn't measure snow on the ground: check the resort webcams and, for ski touring, the Meteomont avalanche bulletin before you set off.
  • Possible ice on the high sections (around 1800 m): lows below zero after precipitation (Sat 28, Sun 29, Mon 30).
  • Strong wind up high: gusts up to 84 km/h on Saturday 28 November.
