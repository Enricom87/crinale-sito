Snow trends · Tuesday 24 November

Snow on the peaks Thursday 26 November, then down to the slopes Friday 27 November

🕒 This article is from 24/11. The forecast for the next few days may have changed since then: the updated one, several times a day, is on the home page and the resort pages. Updated forecast →

In short

  • ❄️ Thursday 26 November: snow on the peaks from about 1300 m up, up to about 43 cm on Lago Scaffaiolo (1775 m).
  • ⛷️ Thursday 26 November: snow on the slopes, 0–20 cm depending on the spot.
  • ⛷️ Friday 27 November: snow on the slopes, 0–3 cm depending on the spot.
  • 🥶 Around Monday 30 November cold is possible but not settled: for now it shows up in about half of the scenarios.
  • 🌍 The big picture, from the Arctic to the stratosphere, makes cold likelier than usual, but the scenarios don't confirm it yet.

The next few days

Thursday 26 November snow reaches the ridge: the highest peaks could pick up about 43 centimetres, with Lago Scaffaiolo (1775 m) among the most generous.

Thursday 26 November the snow comes down to the slopes: up to 20 cm depending on the spot, most at Val di Luce. Friday 27 November adds another up to 3 cm at lift level, most around Cavone. Saturday 28 November adds another up to 3 cm at lift level, most around Passo del Lupo. It's also the most uncertain part of the story: the numbers sharpen with every new model run, and you'll find them spot by spot in the cards.

And the weekend? Saturday 28 November: fresh snow on the peaks from 1200 m up almost everywhere, pick your mountain. Sunday 29 November: good weather pretty much everywhere, pick the handiest spot.

From Sun 29 to Thu 3

From the weekend on, dry days with normal temperatures alternates with dry cold, with temperatures at altitude around zero. The news is the cold: in about half of the scenarios the air at 1500 m drops below −2 °C, especially around Monday 30 November, exactly what snowmaking needs. Snow, on the other hand, is still a gamble: it shows up in about one scenario in ten.

From Fri 4 to Tue 8

Further ahead the tune changes a little: dry, mild days alternates with dry days with normal temperatures, with temperatures at altitude around 2 °C. The cold weakens a bit but doesn't vanish: still in about one scenario in three, especially around Tuesday 8 December. For snow the numbers don't move: still about one scenario in ten, and the day to watch is Tuesday 8 December.

From Wed 9 to Wed 23

Further out the signal weakens, and trends matter more than days: dry days with normal temperatures alternates with dry, mild days, with temperatures at altitude around zero. The cold gains ground: up to about half of the scenarios, best around Thursday 10 December. Snow chances rise: about one scenario in three, and the day to watch is Wednesday 23 December.

Why people are talking about cold

Something rare happened in mid-November: around Tuesday 10 November the stratospheric winds over the Pole reversed. It usually happens in the depths of winter, and never this early. When it does, cold air has more chances to head down towards Europe in the following weeks. The Arctic Oscillation is also dropping, pointing the same way. The Atlantic, on the other hand, gives no clear signal yet. In plain words: cold is more likely than usual between Tuesday 24 November and Tuesday 8 December, but it's not certain, and we don't yet know whether it will reach us or stop further east. For now the models see it in about half of the scenarios: if that number rises in the coming days, here it comes.

How much snow there is now

The official stations don't measure any snow on the ground yet: the season has yet to begin.
