Snow trends · Thursday 26 November

Snow down to the slopes Thursday 26 November

In short

  • ❄️ Thursday 26 November: snow on the peaks from about 1500 m up, up to about 27 cm on Monte Prado (2054 m).
  • ⛷️ Thursday 26 November: snow on the slopes, 0–5 cm depending on the spot.
  • 🥶 Around Saturday 28 November the cold gains strength: for now it shows up in two scenarios in three.
  • 🌍 The big picture, from the Arctic to the stratosphere, points the same way.

The next few days

Thursday 26 November snow reaches the ridge: the highest peaks could pick up about 27 centimetres, with Monte Prado (2054 m) among the most generous.

Thursday 26 November the snow comes down to the slopes: up to 5 cm depending on the spot, most at Passo delle Radici. Spot-by-spot numbers are in the cards.

And the weekend? Saturday 28 November: the best spot is Abetone. Sunday 29 November: good weather pretty much everywhere, pick the handiest spot.

From Tue 1 to Sat 5

From the weekend on, dry days with normal temperatures alternates with dry, mild days, with temperatures at altitude around 2 °C. The news is the cold: in about half of the scenarios the air at 1500 m drops below −2 °C, especially around Tuesday 1 December, exactly what snowmaking needs. For snow, a pause is likely.

From Sun 6 to Thu 10

Further ahead the picture doesn't change much: dry days with normal temperatures alternates with dry, mild days, with temperatures at altitude around zero. The cold stays in the game, still in about one scenario in three, best around Thursday 10 December. Snow, on the other hand, is still a gamble: it shows up in about one scenario in five, and the day to watch is Wednesday 9 December.

From Fri 11 to Fri 25

Further out the signal weakens, and trends matter more than days: dry days with normal temperatures alternates with dry cold, with temperatures at altitude around zero. The cold gains ground: up to about half of the scenarios, best around Saturday 12 December. For snow the numbers don't move: still about one scenario in three, and the day to watch is Saturday 12 December.

Why people are talking about cold

Something rare happened in mid-November: around Thursday 12 November the stratospheric winds over the Pole reversed. It usually happens in the depths of winter, and never this early. When it does, cold air has more chances to head down towards Europe in the following weeks. The Atlantic, on the other hand, gives no clear signal yet. In plain words: cold is more likely than usual between Thursday 26 November and Thursday 10 December, but it's not certain, and we don't yet know whether it will reach us or stop further east. For now the models see it in two scenarios in three: if that number rises in the coming days, here it comes.

How much snow there is now

The official stations don't measure any snow on the ground yet: the season has yet to begin.
