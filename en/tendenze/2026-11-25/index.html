Snow trends · Wednesday 25 November

Snow on the peaks Wednesday 25 November, then down to the slopes Thursday 26 November

🕒 This article is from 25/11. The forecast for the next few days may have changed since then: the updated one, several times a day, is on the home page and the resort pages. Updated forecast →

In short

  • ❄️ Thursday 26 November: snow on the peaks from about 1500 m up, up to about 28 cm on Monte Prado (2054 m).
  • ⛷️ Thursday 26 November: snow on the slopes, 0–8 cm depending on the spot.
  • ⛷️ Saturday 28 November: snow on the slopes, 0–3 cm depending on the spot.
  • 🥶 Around Friday 11 December the cold gains strength: for now it shows up in two scenarios in three.
  • 🌍 The big picture, from the Arctic to the stratosphere, points the same way.

The next few days

Wednesday 25 November snow reaches the ridge: the highest peaks could pick up about 28 centimetres, with Monte Prado (2054 m) among the most generous.

Thursday 26 November the snow comes down to the slopes: up to 8 cm depending on the spot, most at Passo del Lupo. Saturday 28 November adds another up to 3 cm at lift level, most around Passo del Lupo. A word of caution, though: for Thursday 26 November the high-resolution models are much more pessimistic, and at slope level they mostly see rain or snow that doesn't settle. That's why the ranges on the cards start from just a few centimetres. It's also the most uncertain part of the story: the numbers sharpen with every new model run, and you'll find them spot by spot in the cards.

And the weekend? Saturday 28 November: fresh snow on the peaks from 1100 m up almost everywhere, pick your mountain. Sunday 29 November: good weather pretty much everywhere, pick the handiest spot.

From Mon 30 to Fri 4

From the weekend on, dry, mild days alternates with dry days with normal temperatures, with temperatures at altitude around 3 °C. The news is the cold: in about one scenario in three the air at 1500 m drops below −2 °C, especially around Tuesday 1 December, exactly what snowmaking needs. For snow, a pause is likely.

From Sat 5 to Wed 9

Further ahead the picture doesn't change much: dry, mild days alternates with dry days with normal temperatures, with temperatures at altitude around 2 °C. The cold stays in the game, still in about half of the scenarios, best around Wednesday 9 December. Snow, on the other hand, is still a gamble: it shows up in about one scenario in five, and the day to watch is Wednesday 9 December.

From Thu 10 to Thu 24

Further out the signal weakens, and trends matter more than days: dry days with normal temperatures alternates with dry cold, with temperatures at altitude around zero. The cold gains ground: up to two scenarios in three, best around Friday 11 December. For snow the numbers don't move: still about one scenario in five, and the day to watch is Thursday 24 December.

Why people are talking about cold

Something rare happened in mid-November: around Wednesday 11 November the stratospheric winds over the Pole reversed. It usually happens in the depths of winter, and never this early. When it does, cold air has more chances to head down towards Europe in the following weeks. The Atlantic, on the other hand, gives no clear signal yet. In plain words: cold is more likely than usual between Wednesday 25 November and Wednesday 9 December, but it's not certain, and we don't yet know whether it will reach us or stop further east. For now the models see it in two scenarios in three: if that number rises in the coming days, here it comes.

How much snow there is now

The official stations don't measure any snow on the ground yet: the season has yet to begin.
