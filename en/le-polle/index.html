How often are the forecasts updated?

Five times a day, with the latest model runs. The first 5 days come from a physical calculation built for the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, the following days from about 300 scenarios from the main weather centres.

Can you drive up without surprises?

🛞 From 15 November to 15 April, winter tyres or chains on board are required on SS12 «dell'Abetone e del Brennero», SS12 Dir «Variante di Pievepelago» (Anas order), and outside these dates too if it snows or there is ice. On provincial and local roads the owner's order applies: the requirement is always signposted.

Clear road: the models see no snow or ice for the next 5 days. The chains can stay in the boot.
